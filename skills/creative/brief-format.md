# Creative Brief Format Specification

Every creative brief produced by Lorewise must be falsifiable, linked to an open bet or playbook lesson, and structured for immediate designer or video creator execution.

---

## 1. Specification Schema

```markdown
# Creative Brief: <slug>-<brief_id>

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Client:** <slug>
**Objective:** purchases | leads
**Associated Bet:** <bet_id_or_none>
**Target Platform:** Meta | Google | TikTok
**Target Format:** 1:1 Feed Image | 9:16 Reel/Story Video | 4:5 Carousel

---

### 1. Strategic Hypothesis
- **Hypothesis:** If we test <angle_or_hook> against <target_audience>, then CPA will be $\le <target_cpa>$ because <rationale>.
- **Playbook Lesson Reference:** <lesson_id_or_none>

### 2. Audience & Awareness Stage
- **Funnel Stage:** Cold Problem-Aware | Solution-Aware | Product-Aware
- **Customer Desire:** Primary trigger motivating purchase.
- **Key Objection:** Barrier preventing conversion.

### 3. Hook Variations (Mandatory 3 Options)
1. **Hook A (Problem-First):** Visual and text opening highlighting pain point.
2. **Hook B (Social Proof):** Customer quote, rating badge, or before-after reveal.
3. **Hook C (Curiosity/Contrast):** Counter-intuitive claim or unexpected demonstration.

### 4. Body & Visual Direction
- **Visual Style:** Clean studio photography / raw smartphone UGC / illustrated breakdown.
- **Color & Palette:** High-contrast background with prominent brand accent.
- **Product Presentation:** Clear packaging shot with legible benefit callouts.

### 5. Messaging & Copy Assets
- **Primary Text Option 1:** Concise benefit-driven copy ($\le 125$ characters).
- **Primary Text Option 2:** Story-driven customer transformation.
- **Headline (Display):** Bold 4–7 word benefit hook.
- **Call to Action (Button):** "Shop Now" / "Get Offer".

### 6. Technical & Acceptance Criteria
- **Aspect Ratios:** 1:1 (1080x1080 px) and 9:16 (1080x1920 px).
- **File Format:** High-res PNG (static) or MP4 H.264 (video).
- **Text Safety Margin:** Keep text within central 80% safe zone.
- **Receipt Verification:** No fabricated claims or unverified statistics.
```

---

## 2. Example: Brief BR-demo-shop-014

```markdown
# Creative Brief: demo-shop-BR-014

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Client:** demo-shop
**Objective:** purchases
**Associated Bet:** B-014
**Target Platform:** Meta
**Target Format:** 1:1 Static Feed Image & 9:16 Story

---

### 1. Strategic Hypothesis
- **Hypothesis:** Reallocating budget to Lookalike audiences with UGC-style social proof will maintain CPA under $36.00 by resetting creative fatigue from exhausted broad targeting.
- **Playbook Lesson Reference:** L-019

### 2. Audience & Awareness Stage
- **Funnel Stage:** Solution-Aware (Skincare buyers seeking lasting hydration).
- **Customer Desire:** All-day barrier hydration without greasy residue.
- **Key Objection:** "Most serums dry out or irritate my skin after two hours."

### 3. Hook Variations
1. **Hook A (Review Quote):** "I threw away my $80 moisturizer after 3 days of this."
2. **Hook B (Dermatologist Angle):** "What actually happens to your skin barrier when you switch to ceramide mist."
3. **Hook C (Visual Demonstration):** Side-by-side skin hydration sensor reading (Before vs 8 Hours Later).

### 4. Body & Visual Direction
- **Visual Style:** UGC aesthetic; natural bathroom lighting, unedited skin texture.
- **Product Focus:** Product held in hand with texture dropper visible.

### 5. Messaging & Copy Assets
- **Primary Text:** "Over 1,200 women switched this month. 100% clean ceramides that lock in moisture for 24 hours straight."
- **Headline:** Real Barrier Repair That Lasts
- **Call to Action:** Shop Now

### 6. Technical & Acceptance Criteria
- **Dimensions:** 1080x1080 px (1:1) and 1080x1920 px (9:16).
- **Format:** PNG with sRGB color profile.
- **Compliance:** Zero medical cure claims; authentic customer phrasing only.
```
