# Direct Response Copy Frameworks

## Core Frameworks Comparison Table

| Framework | Meaning | When to Use | Structure | Example Skeleton |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PAS** | Problem - Agitate - Solve | Universal, highly effective for pain-driven markets. | Identify pain → Make it hurt more → Present your product as the cure. | "Back pain? It ruins your sleep and limits your day. Our ergonomic chair aligns your spine so you wake up refreshed." |
| **AIDA** | Attention - Interest - Desire - Action | Long-form copy, emails, landing pages. | Hook them → Share fascinating facts → Show benefits → Tell them what to do. | "Stop wasting ad spend. 90% of buyers overpay. Our AI cuts costs by 30%. Click here to start your free trial." |
| **BAB** | Before - After - Bridge | High-aspiration markets (fitness, wealth). | Show the current bad state → Paint the dream future state → Your product is the bridge. | "Drowning in spreadsheets? Imagine one dashboard with all your data. Our software connects it all." |
| **4P** | Promise - Picture - Proof - Push | Persuasive sales letters, high ticket. | Bold claim → Visualize the outcome → Back it up with data/testimonials → Call to action. | "Double your open rates. See your revenue spike. Join 5,000+ marketers who did. Buy the course now." |
| **FAB** | Feature - Advantage - Benefit | E-commerce product descriptions. | What it is → What it does better → Why the user should care. | "Made of titanium. It's lighter than steel. You can carry it all day without fatigue." |
| **PASTOR** | Problem - Amplify - Story - Transformation - Offer - Response | Webinars, VSLs, complex sales. | PAS + narrative journey + specific offer details + clear CTA. | "Low leads? It kills growth. I struggled too, until I found this method. Now I generate 100/day. Get my blueprint here. Click buy." |

## Objection Neutralization Patterns

When reviewing copy, ensure one or more of these 6 core objections are neutralized:

1. **"Too expensive"** 
   *Pattern:* Value reframing, ROI calculation, payment splitting.
   *Example:* "For less than the cost of your daily coffee..." or "It pays for itself after the first two conversions."
2. **"No time"** 
   *Pattern:* Time-saving proof, quick-start path.
   *Example:* "Setup takes under 5 minutes. No coding required."
3. **"Don't trust you"** 
   *Pattern:* Social proof, guarantee, risk reversal.
   *Example:* "Trusted by 10,000+ brands. Try it risk-free for 30 days or your money back."
4. **"Don't need it right now"** 
   *Pattern:* Problem awareness, cost of inaction.
   *Example:* "Every day you wait, you're leaking 20% of your traffic to competitors."
5. **"Need to think about it"** 
   *Pattern:* Scarcity, comparison simplification.
   *Example:* "Only 50 spots remaining. Here's exactly how we compare to the alternative."
6. **"Tried something like this before"** 
   *Pattern:* Differentiation, unique mechanism.
   *Example:* "Other diets fail because they ignore macros. Our unique metabolic tracking changes that."
