# Quality Gates

Before any campaign or creative goes live, it must pass these quality gates. 

**CRITICAL RULE:** A single failed MUST-HAVE item prevents delivery. Do not launch until resolved.

## Copy Quality Gate (50-Point Checklist)

### A. Strategic Alignment
- [ ] Offer matches the defined Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
- [ ] Positioning is clear and distinct from competitors.
- [ ] Unique mechanism is explicitly stated.
- [ ] Tone of voice matches brand guidelines.
- [ ] Pain point is accurately identified.

### B. Headline & Hook
- [ ] Makes a specific, tangible promise.
- [ ] Uses numbers or concrete proof elements.
- [ ] Creates a curiosity gap without using clickbait.
- [ ] Immediately relevant to the target audience.
- [ ] Visual hook (if video/image) matches the text hook.

### C. Body Copy
- [ ] Focuses on benefits rather than just features (So What?).
- [ ] Integrates social proof naturally.
- [ ] Anticipates and handles primary objections.
- [ ] Maintains a logical flow from hook to pitch.
- [ ] Uses formatting (bullet points, short paragraphs) for readability.

### D. CTA (Call to Action)
- [ ] Clear, unmistakable next step.
- [ ] Only one primary action requested.
- [ ] Injects a reason to act now (urgency/scarcity).
- [ ] Includes risk reversal (guarantee, free trial).
- [ ] Button text matches the copy promise.

### E. Platform Compliance
- [ ] Adheres to character limits (truncation considered).
- [ ] No prohibited claims (e.g., guaranteed income, deceptive weight loss).
- [ ] Avoids platform-specific flagged trigger words.
- [ ] Image text is under 20% (if applicable/best practice).
- [ ] URLs match the display domain.

### F. Grammar & Style
- [ ] Active voice used over passive voice.
- [ ] No industry jargon (unless specifically targeting experts).
- [ ] Reading level appropriate for the audience (typically 6th-8th grade).
- [ ] No spelling or grammatical errors.
- [ ] Consistent capitalization and punctuation.

### G. Proof & Evidence
- [ ] All numerical claims are backed by verifiable data.
- [ ] Testimonials are real and properly sourced.
- [ ] Case studies are relevant to the avatar.
- [ ] Authority markers (badges, features) are valid.
- [ ] Timeframes for results are realistic.

### H. Format & Layout
- [ ] Scannable by someone scrolling quickly.
- [ ] Sufficient whitespace between concepts.
- [ ] Visual hierarchy guides the eye to the CTA.
- [ ] Emojis used strategically, not overwhelmingly.
- [ ] Mobile-optimized preview checked.

### I. Conversion Architecture
- [ ] Message match: Ad copy directly aligns with landing page headline.
- [ ] Friction minimized: No unnecessary steps implied.
- [ ] Value proposition is restated near the CTA.
- [ ] Scent trail is maintained visually and textually.
- [ ] Intent matches the funnel stage (Cold vs. Retargeting).

*(Note: While these are grouped into 9 sections and 45 points are listed for brevity, the remaining 5 subjective checks rely on the Media Buyer's gut-check of emotional resonance, rhythm, contrast, surprise, and platform native-feel.)*

## Strategy Quality Gate (40-Point Checklist)

### A. Market Understanding
- [ ] Total Addressable Market (TAM) is defined.
- [ ] Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) is documented.
- [ ] Competitor map is completed and reviewed.
- [ ] Primary market alternatives identified.
- [ ] Macro-economic factors considered.

### B. Offer Architecture
- [ ] Value proposition is clear and concise.
- [ ] Pricing is justified against alternatives.
- [ ] Risk reversal mechanism is in place.
- [ ] Bonuses or urgency levers are defined.
- [ ] Margin supports acquisition cost.

### C. Channel Strategy
- [ ] Channel selection rationale is documented.
- [ ] Budget allocation logic is sound.
- [ ] Platform intent matches offer intent.
- [ ] Creative formats align with channel best practices.
- [ ] Cross-channel frequency caps are planned.

### D. Funnel Design
- [ ] All stages (Top, Mid, Bottom) are defined.
- [ ] Tracking pixels/API are verified complete.
- [ ] Primary bottleneck is hypothesized.
- [ ] Drop-off points are instrumented for measurement.
- [ ] Post-purchase flow is active.

### E. Measurement Framework
- [ ] Primary KPIs are explicitly defined.
- [ ] Attribution model is agreed upon.
- [ ] Reporting cadence is scheduled.
- [ ] Blended vs. In-platform targets are set.
- [ ] LTV tracking mechanism is in place.

### F. Creative Strategy
- [ ] Hook catalog is generated.
- [ ] Format mix (Video, Static, Carousel) is planned.
- [ ] Testing roadmap (Variables isolated) is ready.
- [ ] Competitor creative swipe file is referenced.
- [ ] Asset refresh schedule is established.

### G. Budget & Economics
- [ ] Unit economics are calculated.
- [ ] Break-even CPA and ROAS are known.
- [ ] Margin requirements are documented.
- [ ] Scaling thresholds are predefined.
- [ ] Cash flow constraints are verified.

### H. Risk Assessment
- [ ] Downside scenarios are modeled.
- [ ] Stop rules (when to kill an ad) are defined.
- [ ] Rollback plan for failed tests is ready.
- [ ] Account ban mitigation plan is in place.
- [ ] Inventory/capacity limits are confirmed.
