# Video Creative System

A systematic approach to generating, testing, and optimizing high-converting video ad formats across paid social channels.

## 9 High-Converting Video Ad Formats

| Format | Focus | Optimal Length | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. UGC Testimonial** | Authentic customer story and experience. | 15-45s | Mid/Bottom Funnel, Building Trust |
| **2. Problem-Solution** | Explicit pain point followed by the product fix. | 15-30s | Broad/Top Funnel |
| **3. Before-After Transformation** | Visual evidence of change/results. | 15-30s | Health, Beauty, Software |
| **4. Talking Head Expert** | Authority figure explaining value/mechanics. | 30-60s | Complex B2B, Info-products |
| **5. Product Demo / Unboxing** | Tactile, detailed look at the physical product. | 15-45s | E-commerce, Physical Goods |
| **6. Tutorial / How-To** | Step-by-step value demonstration. | 30-60s | SaaS, App Installs, Tools |
| **7. Social Proof Montage** | Rapid-fire compilation of reviews/results. | 15-30s | Retargeting |
| **8. Behind-The-Scenes** | Brand transparency, manufacturing process. | 15-30s | Brand Awareness, Apparel |
| **9. Trend-Riding** | Hooking into a current cultural moment/audio. | 10-20s | TikTok/Reels Top Funnel |

## Format Structure & Timing

- **Hook Window (0-3s)**: Capture attention instantly. High energy, pattern interrupt, state the problem, or ask a provocative question.
- **Body Structure (3-15s)**: Deliver the payload. Explain the unique mechanism, provide proof, or demonstrate the product.
- **CTA Placement**: Usually at the end (last 3-5s), but secondary CTAs (e.g., text overlays) should persist throughout the body.

## Modular Script Template

Use this blueprint to structure video scripts systematically:

```text
[0-3s] {hook}: "Tired of [Pain Point]? Here is the secret to [Desired Result]."
[3-8s] {problem}: "Most people try [Old Way], but it fails because of [Root Cause]."
[8-15s] {solution}: "That's why [Product/Service] uses [Unique Mechanism] to fix it."
[15-22s] {proof}: "We helped [Client/Avatar] achieve [Metric] in just [Timeframe]."
[22-30s] {cta}: "Click the link below to get [Offer/Lead Magnet] today."
```

## Fatigue Detection System

Identify where your video is failing to take corrective action:
- **Hook Fatigue (0-3s drop)**: The audience is no longer stopping their scroll. *Action: Swap the first 3 seconds (new visual, new text hook).*
- **Body Fatigue (3-15s drop)**: The hook worked, but the argument failed to retain them. *Action: Condense the script, improve pacing, add b-roll.*
- **Audience Fatigue (Frequency > 3.5)**: The creative is fine, but the audience has seen it too many times. *Action: Expand targeting, refresh creative completely.*

## Testing Hierarchy

Do not test everything at once. Follow this chronological testing order:
1. **Test Hooks First**: Run 3-5 different 3-second hooks with the exact same body and CTA.
2. **Test Body Variations**: Once a winning hook is found, test 2-3 different body scripts (e.g., emotional vs. logical argument).
3. **Test CTA**: Finally, optimize the call-to-action and end-card offer.
