### BLND-FACT-01: Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)
- **Claim:** Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) is calculated as total store gross revenue divided by blended paid marketing expenditure across all active channels.

### BLND-FACT-02: Blended CPA Definition
- **Claim:** Blended Cost Per Acquisition (Blended CPA) is calculated as total media spend across all ad networks divided by total unique orders in the store backend.
- **Source:** [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/256906328261745)
- **Checked:** 2026-09-15 | **Expires:** 2027-09-15

- **Checked:** 2026-09-15 | **Expires:** 2027-09-15
