# Regional Module: Central Asia & CIS Markets

Campaigns targeting emerging markets like Uzbekistan require distinct adaptations in targeting, funnels, and trust signals compared to North American or European standards.

## Uzbekistan Digital Market Context

- **Internet Penetration**: ~89% (over 33M internet users out of ~37M total population).
- **Social Platforms**: 
  - Meta ecosystem: ~16M Facebook users, ~15M Instagram users.
  - Demographic skew: Approximately 67% male.
- **Messaging & Commerce**: **Telegram** is the dominant platform for both communication and commerce. Email marketing and WhatsApp have negligible impact here.
- **Payments**: Global gateways (Stripe, PayPal) are not standard. Local gateways dominate: **Click**, **Payme**, and **Uzum**. Cash on delivery is also prevalent.
- **Language**: Trilingual market. Ad copy must navigate Uzbek (Latin script), Russian, and English. Code-switching is common, and language preference heavily segments the audience.
- **Trust Signals**: Users look for different authority markers. A verified physical address, a local phone number, and a highly active Telegram channel with community comments are stronger trust signals than standard website reviews.

## Funnel Architecture for CIS Markets

Traditional landing page funnels often fail here. Optimize for conversational commerce and speed.

- **Primary Funnel (Conversational)**: 
  Ad → Telegram Bot / Channel → Qualification Flow → Direct Sales Call or Chat Closing.
- **Secondary Funnel (Hybrid)**: 
  Ad → Minimal Landing Page → Redirect to Telegram Group/Bot → Sale.
- **Follow-up**: Email has critically low penetration. Utilize SMS and Telegram notifications for retargeting and follow-up.
- **Speed-to-Lead**: Absolute priority. Response times must be <5 minutes. Waiting longer typically results in an 80% drop in closing probability.

## Platform Benchmarks (Uzbekistan)

- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)**: CPMs are significantly lower than Tier 1 markets. Expect CPMs to range between $2.00 to $8.00 depending on the specific niche and audience wealth tier.
- **Google Ads**: Search inventory can be limited for specific local queries. However, **YouTube pre-roll** ads are highly effective for mass reach and brand building.
- **Telegram Ads**: Official Telegram Ads operate on a CPM model (minimum typically around $2 CPM). Highly effective for precise channel-based targeting.
