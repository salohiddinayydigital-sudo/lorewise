# Targeting & Audience Architecture Platform Reference

Official reference rules and platform mechanics for audience targeting, auction overlap, Advantage+ audience expansion, and exclusion hygiene.

## Fact Index

### TGT-FACT-01: Auction Overlap Mechanics
- **Claim:** Meta Auction Overlap occurs when multiple ad sets from the same account compete in the same auction, causing the platform to suppress the lower-performing ad set.
- **Source:** [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/166299144065266)
- **Checked:** 2026-09-15 | **Expires:** 2027-09-15

### TGT-FACT-02: Advantage+ Audience Expansion Scope
- **Claim:** Advantage+ audience dynamically expands reach beyond specified audience suggestions unless strict boundaries are enforced in Audience Controls.
- **Source:** [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/701625841444222)
- **Checked:** 2026-09-15 | **Expires:** 2027-09-15

### TGT-FACT-03: First-Time Impression Ratio (Audience Saturation)
- **Claim:** First-time impression ratio measures the percentage of impressions delivered to people who have not previously seen an ad from the ad set.
- **Source:** [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/279867999371077)
- **Checked:** 2026-09-15 | **Expires:** 2027-09-15

### TGT-FACT-04: Google Customer Match List Threshold
- **Claim:** Google Ads Customer Match requires first-party hashed customer contact data and enforces a minimum threshold of 1,000 active matched users for Search and YouTube.
- **Source:** [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6379332)
- **Checked:** 2026-09-15 | **Expires:** 2027-09-15

### TGT-FACT-05: Purchaser Exclusion Hygiene
- **Claim:** Custom audience exclusions for recent purchasers prevent prospecting campaign budgets from paying acquisition costs on repeat customers.
- **Source:** [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1051521768297746)
- **Checked:** 2026-09-15 | **Expires:** 2027-09-15
