# Competitor Ad Angle Extraction Methodology

A structured protocol for extracting actionable marketing intelligence from public ad transparency libraries (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center, TikTok Creative Center).

---

## 1. Ethical & Terms-Compliant Discovery Protocol

1. **Rely on Official Public Surfaces Only:**
   - Meta Ad Library (`facebook.com/ads/library`)
   - Google Ads Transparency Center (`adstransparency.google.com`)
   - TikTok Creative Center (`ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter`)
2. **Never Scrape Authenticated Surfaces:**
   - Do not bypass authentication gates, run headless scrapers against private portals, or violate platform terms of service.
3. **No Direct Copying:**
   - Extract underlying psychological angles, mechanisms, and structural formulas. Never copy protected trade dress, logos, scripts verbatim, or creative imagery.

---

## 2. The 5-Point Extraction Framework

When auditing a competitor's public ads, extract five distinct dimensions:

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. LONGEVITY (The Scaling Proxy)                             │
│    - Look for ads with the earliest "Started running" date.  │
│    - In paid media, losing ads are paused within days.       │
│    - Ads running 30–90+ days are virtually always profitable │
│      scaling winners for that brand.                         │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. VISUAL HOOK TAXONOMY (The First 3 Seconds)                │
│    - What is on screen in frame 1?                           │
│    - Pattern interrupt? Extreme close-up? Shocking headline? │
│    - Split screen comparison? UGC creator unboxing?          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. CORE PROMISE & ANGLE                                      │
│    - Which of the 6 hook families is being deployed?         │
│    - What specific pain point is highlighted?                │
│    - Is the angle fear-based, aspiration-based, or logic?    │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. THE MECHANISM EXPLAINED                                   │
│    - How does the competitor claim their product works?      │
│    - What buzzword or proprietary term do they use?          │
│    - Is the mechanism credible or vulnerable to scrutiny?    │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. THE OFFER ARCHITECTURE                                    │
│    - Entry price point, discount depth (e.g. 20% off vs BOGO)│
│    - Risk reversal terms (30-day money-back guarantee)       │
│    - Shipping thresholds ($50+ free shipping)                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
