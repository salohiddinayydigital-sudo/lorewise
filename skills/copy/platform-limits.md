# Advertising Platform Copy Specifications & Character Limits

A reference guide for character boundaries, asset ratios, and copy formatting constraints across supported paid advertising platforms.

---

## 1. Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

| Element | Recommended Limit | Technical Hard Limit | Notes & Best Practices |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Text** | $\le 125$ chars (Short)<br>or 250–500 chars (Story) | 2,200 chars | Truncates with "...See more" after 125 chars on mobile feed. Hook must appear before truncation. |
| **Headline** | $\le 40$ chars | 255 chars | Truncates on mobile if longer than 40–45 characters. Keep punchy. |
| **Description** | $\le 30$ chars | 255 chars | Displays primarily on Desktop feeds and specific placements. Often omitted on mobile. |
| **Display Link** | $\le 30$ chars | Domain root | Custom path display (e.g. `brand.com/special-offer`). |
| **Aspect Ratios** | Feed: 1:1 or 4:5<br>Reels/Stories: 9:16 | — | Keep safe zones: avoid text in top 14% and bottom 20% of 9:16 assets. |

---

## 2. Google Ads Responsive Search Ads (RSA)

| Element | Minimum Count | Maximum Count | Character Limit | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Headlines** | 3 required | Up to 15 headlines | Strictly $\le 30$ characters each | Include 3 brand/product terms, 5 benefits/mechanisms, 4 call-to-actions, and 3 keyword insertion variations. |
| **Descriptions** | 2 required | Up to 4 descriptions | Strictly $\le 90$ characters each | Detail proof points, shipping/trial guarantees, and concrete value propositions. |
| **Display URL Paths** | 0 | 2 subpaths | $\le 15$ characters per path | e.g. `domain.com/Category/Offer` |

---

## 3. Telegram Sponsored Ads

| Element | Limit | Policy Constraints |
|---|---|---|
| **Ad Text** | Strictly $\le 160$ characters | No excessive capital letters, no multiple exclamation marks, no spam emoji. |
| **Destination Link** | 1 link | Must route to a public Telegram channel (`t.me/channel`) or official Telegram bot (`t.me/bot`). |
| **Tone** | Informative & concise | No sensationalist claims, clickbait phrasing, or exaggerated promises. |

---

## 4. TikTok Ads

| Element | Recommended Limit | Technical Limit | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ad Display Text** | $\le 100$ characters | 100 characters | Overlays bottom of screen. Keep concise so UI buttons (like, share, comment) do not obscure text. |
| **Video Dimensions** | 9:16 vertical | $720 \times 1280$ min | Vertical full-screen video only. |
| **Audio** | 100% required | Background music / voiceover | Sound-on environment. Hook requires both visual movement and audio dialogue. |
